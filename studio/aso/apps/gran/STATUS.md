## Decisions

- Headline 4 in Polish reads "Każde podejście widać już w domu" instead of the planned "Każde podejście widać przed wyjściem": the planned line broke into three lines with a one word last line at the set's headline size, and the new line keeps the benefit (see the climbs before leaving home).
- App Store headlines are set at 41 CSS px (123 px in the file) on two lines, so no headline in either language ends with a single word; Play headlines at 27 CSS px. The same size runs through the whole set; there are no per language layout overrides.
- The landscape trail enters the map on the first screenshot as a red approach segment, so the line in the picture continues on the screen; on the map the route then switches to the black trail like the real marked route.
- The sun sits once, low over the open slope of frame 3. A panorama is one picture, so a second sun for the sunset of frame 6 would break it; frame 6 gets the warm sunset band instead.
- Mountain huts stay unnamed; places, peaks and heights are real (Biały Jar, Kocioł Łomniczki, Przełęcz pod Śnieżką, Śnieżka 1603 m), trail colours and times are sample data.
